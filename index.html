Bachelor’s · Semester 7 Two Methods for an Inverted Pendulum PID · State feedback

I returned to the inverted-pendulum project for two courses and applied two different control approaches. It became one of the most interesting projects I worked on at Shahid Beheshti University.

PID controller

For Industrial Control, we implemented a PID controller. The physical system required us to adjust the coefficients from their simulated values to get a better result.

State-feedback controller

For Modern Control Systems, we implemented a state-feedback controller with an observer to estimate the system’s four states. The controller worked, although the simulation performed better.

Bachelor’s · Summer after Semester 6 Traffic Sign Recognition Research Computer vision · Internship

During an internship in Iran, I researched image recognition for traffic signs. I also completed Coursera coursework in deep learning, mathematics for machine learning, and Python.

Bachelor’s · Semester 5 Inverted Pendulum Arduino · LQR control

For Fundamentals of Mechatronics, we built an inverted pendulum and used an Arduino board to apply an LQR controller. The result gave me a reason to revisit and improve the system during my seventh semester.

Bachelor’s · Semester 6 Wind Turbine Simulator Arduino · PLC · NI LabVIEW

For Digital Control Systems, a teammate and I controlled the output voltage of a wind-turbine simulator. We used Arduino to read a ZMPT101B sensor and communicate with a PLC, then designed a PID controller in NI LabVIEW to keep the voltage within a small, safe range.

Bachelor’s · Semester 6 Application Chance Calculator Machine learning · Python

Bachelor’s · Semester 5 Half-Bridge Inverter AVR · PWM · MPWM

For Power Electronics, my team and I built a half-bridge inverter as an optional project. The most interesting part for me was using CodeVisionAVR on an ATmega8 to apply PWM and MPWM.
